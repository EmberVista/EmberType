# EmberType 1.1 (build 207) — Release Log

**Why:** four paying customers (Apr–Sep 2026) asked to dictate their own punctuation and to insert words mid-sentence without a stray capital and period. Details and tests: `DevHarness/README.md`.

**Scope:** "Spoken punctuation only" setting (off by default), mid-sentence insert fix in normal mode, single quotes, greater/less than sign, Ollama "No models installed" label, "Add space after paste" fix. Version 1.0.2/206 → 1.1/207.
