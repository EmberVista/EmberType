EmberType 1.1: Spoken punctuation

Say your own punctuation. Turn on Spoken punctuation only and EmberType types only the punctuation you say, and adds none of its own.

To turn it on: open EmberType → AI Models in the sidebar → click the gear icon at the right of the model filters → switch on Spoken punctuation only.

Then say: comma, period, question mark, exclamation point, colon, semicolon, open paren / close paren, open quote / close quote, hyphen, em dash, new line, new paragraph.

"dear Sam comma thanks for the notes period can we talk friday question mark"
→ Dear Sam, thanks for the notes. Can we talk Friday?

Full list and tips: embertype.com/docs/spoken-punctuation
